Residential construction

New houses and multi-storey homes, built from approved drawings to handover.

Overview

Whether you are building your first family home or a two-storey residence with rental units, we manage the full build: site preparation, foundations, structure, roofing, services and finishes.

Scope of work

  • Site clearing, setting out and excavation
  • Foundations, columns, beams and slabs
  • Masonry, plastering and roofing
  • Electrical and plumbing installation
  • Tiling, painting, doors and windows
  • Final cleaning and handover inspection

Who this is for

Homeowners and families planning a new house or an extension.

How the project runs

  1. Brief and site visit. We understand your needs and inspect the site.
  2. Itemised quotation. You receive a BOQ-based price and programme.
  3. Agreement. Scope, payments and timeline are agreed in writing.
  4. Construction. Work proceeds stage by stage with regular updates.
  5. Handover. Final inspection, documents and a walkthrough.

What to prepare before you contact us

  • Drawings or sketches, if you have them
  • Site location and land size
  • Your preferred start date and budget range
